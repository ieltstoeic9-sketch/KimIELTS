Script

Helen: How do you spend your day, Tom?

Tom: Well, right now, I'm in high school, but I also have a part-time job. So I'm pretty busy. I get up early, around 6:00 a.m. (Question 1) Then I work from seven until nine (Question 2). I go to school at 9:30, and I study until 4:00 p.m. (Question 3) Then I work again from five until seven (Question 4).

Helen: So what do you do?

Tom: I'm a dog walker.

Helen: What? What's that?

Tom: A dog walker. I take people's dogs for walks. It's great, and it keeps me in good shape, too.

Helen: Then, what do you do after seven?

Tom: I have supper at 7:30 (Question 5). I start to do my homework at 8:00. It often takes about an hour and a half to finish the work (Question 6), then I watch TV for about two hours (Question 7). I go to bed at 11:30 (Question 8).

Helen: Your day is hard, the same as mine is.
